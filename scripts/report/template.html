What was held constant.

Design

Score definition

For each scenario, shortest-path/fixed is the reference at 100.

score = 100 × [
  0.40 × completion ratio
+ 0.40 × reference time / system time
+ 0.20 × reference waiting / system waiting
]

Completion ratio divides the system's completion fraction by the reference fraction. Time is summed horizon-truncated time from scheduled departure to completion or 75 seconds. Waiting includes stopped cars and external queues; one second per requested trip is added to both waiting totals before taking the ratio. Each ratio is capped at 2. Overall and breakdown scores are unweighted means of scenario scores.

This composite uses subjective weights and is not a standard transport metric. The underlying completion, waiting and time measures are provided separately. Completed-only mean and p95 are descriptive and do not determine the score.

Boundaries of this first pass

  • One demand seed, no repetitions, uncertainty intervals or statistical significance claims.
  • Short 75-second horizon, with demand only through 45 seconds. Unfinished journeys are right-censored at the horizon.
  • Two API checkpoints, at 20s and 40s, rather than the interactive app's frequent requests. Signal plans expire after 20 seconds without refresh.
  • Barrier mode pauses simulation during API calls. This isolates decision quality and does not measure real-time latency penalties.
  • All methods get local closure safety repairs. API routing is restricted to legal local candidates and a 15% cost-improvement rule.
  • No pedestrians, turning conflicts, lane changes, learned driver compliance or real-world traffic calibration.
  • One direction blocked means one entire direction is closed, not a reduction from two lanes to one.
  • Comparisons across maps describe different synthetic systems; ranking uses within-scenario normalisation.
